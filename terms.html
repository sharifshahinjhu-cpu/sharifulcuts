1. What I offer

I offer two services. Authority Shorts: 12, 20 or 30 edited vertical shorts a month, in 9:16 and (by plan) 4:5. Ad Creative Sprint: one ad concept, 4 hooks, 9:16 and 4:5 (8 files). The deliverables, prices and rules for each are the ones published on Authority Shorts and Ad Creative Sprint on the date of your order, and I confirm them in writing before work starts.

2. What you provide

Work starts, and the delivery clock runs, only when your input is complete: every “blocker” on the offer’s input list has passed. I review your intake within one business day and send one message listing blockers. “Warnings” don’t stop the work; I proceed and note them in writing. You must own, or have permission for, all footage, audio, images and other material you send, and the people appearing in it must have agreed to its use and editing. For ads, you are responsible for claims, especially on health and financial topics, and for any creator’s permission to use their content in paid social.

3. Delivery, feedback and closing

“Delivery” means I send you a 720p preview with a watermark. Authority Shorts: weekly batches, each within 5 business days (Monday to Friday) of complete input. Ad Creative Sprint: within 72 hours of complete input. Your feedback is due within 5 business days (Monday to Friday) of the preview; after that the clock stops, and 14 days without a reply closes the batch or sprint. These are my current commitments and may be updated for new orders after my first test jobs.

4. Revisions and new requests

A revision changes something inside the agreed deliverables and your original brief. Changing the brief is a new request. One round is one combined feedback message per batch or sprint. Authority Shorts: 1–2 rounds per batch; Ad Creative Sprint: 1 round per sprint. Extra rounds: USD 5 per short (Authority Shorts) or USD 45 per sprint (Ad Creative Sprint).

  • The invoice amount must arrive in full: bank and transfer charges are paid by the sender.

  • Payment method: international bank wire (primary). I can’t take direct card payments; if you prefer to pay by card, you can hire me through Upwork instead (same scope, with the same 50/50 split as fixed-price milestones, under Upwork’s own payment rules).

Monthly plans

  • Monthly plans are paid 100% in advance, at the start of each monthly cycle. The cycle starts when intake is complete and the payment has arrived.
  • Weekly batches are sent as watermarked previews; the clean finals are released once the month’s payment has arrived.
  • The invoice for the next cycle is sent 7 days before it starts. If it hasn’t arrived, work on the next cycle does not begin.
  • There is no fixed term. Cancel any time by telling me before the next invoice. Unused shorts do not roll over to the next month.
  • Scale (30 shorts a month) is limited to one client at a time.
  • If more than 7 days of payment is overdue, work pauses.

The 3-sprint pack is valid for 90 days from payment. Prices may change for new clients; existing monthly clients keep their price for 3 months after a change.

6. Refunds

  • Before work starts: 100% refund.
  • After work starts, before I send the preview: refund minus completed work (for a sprint, at least 50% is kept; for a monthly plan, in proportion to the shorts already delivered).
  • After I send the preview: no refund.
  • If I fail to deliver the agreed deliverables on time, you may choose a refund of the undelivered part.

7. Ownership and rights

After full payment, you own the final delivered videos. My templates, tools and system code remain mine. I use only music, sound effects and fonts that are my own or properly licensed for your commercial use. I will not show your work publicly, including in a portfolio, unless you give separate written consent; it is off by default.

8. No guarantee of results

I guarantee the deliverables, the specification and the delivery time — not views, sales or ad results. I’m not responsible for decisions made by platforms, such as ad rejections or account actions.

9. Liability

To the extent permitted by law, my total liability for a batch or sprint is limited to the amount you paid for that batch or sprint. Neither of us is liable for indirect or consequential damages.

10. Your files and data

Raw footage is deleted 30 days after final delivery. Final files and project files are kept for 12 months in cloud storage (not on a local disk). I don’t sell your material or publish it to third parties. My production is system-assisted and supervised by me. More in the privacy policy.

11. Capacity and subcontracting

I work individually and keep capacity limited: at most 2 active monthly plans and 1 sprint at a time. If I’m full, I’ll tell you before you pay. If I involve another editor, I’ll tell you first.

12. Governing law and changes

These terms are governed by the laws of the People’s Republic of Bangladesh unless a specific written agreement with you says otherwise. I may update these terms; changes apply to orders placed after the “Last updated” date, and the version on the date of your order applies to you.
