FAQ

What counts as “delivery”?

Delivery means I send you a 720p preview with a watermark. Once the remaining balance has arrived, I release the clean final files. My turnaround promise counts until the preview is sent; your feedback window and revision rounds start from that day.

When does the delivery clock start?

When your input is complete: every blocker on the input list has passed. I review your intake within one business day and send one message listing anything missing. Warnings don’t stop the work — I proceed and note them in writing.

What is a revision and what is a new request?

A revision changes something inside the agreed deliverables and your original brief (caption timing, swapping B-roll, colour fixes, audio level, trimming). Changing the brief itself — a new hook, a new offer, a different format or style — is a new request and is priced as an add-on or a new sprint. One round means one combined feedback message per batch or sprint. Mistakes on my side are fixed free and don’t use a round.

How and when do I pay?

Monthly plans are paid 100% in advance each month. The pilot and single sprints are paid 50% to start and 50% before the final files are released. The 3-sprint pack is paid in full up front. Payment instructions are sent only after you confirm my written summary. Payment is by international bank wire in USD; bank charges are paid by the sender. If you’d rather pay by card, hire me through Upwork (same scope, milestone payments).

What if I want a refund?
  • Before work starts: 100% refund.
  • After work starts, before I send the preview: refund minus completed work (for a sprint, at least 50% is kept; for a monthly plan, in proportion to the shorts already delivered).
  • After I send the preview: no refund.
  • If I fail to deliver the agreed deliverables on time, you may choose a refund of the undelivered part.
Do you use AI tools?

Yes. I edit with AI-assisted tools, and every file is checked against your brief and the written spec before it is sent. Your footage and files are never sold or published to third parties.

Do you write scripts, hooks or ad copy, or post for me?

No. I edit what you give me. I don’t write scripts, hooks or ad copy, I don’t post to your accounts, and (for ads) I don’t run or test campaigns.

Can you guarantee views, sales or ad results?

No. I guarantee the deliverables, the specification and the delivery time. Results depend on your content, offer and audience, and platform decisions (ad rejections, account actions) are outside my control.

Will you show my work publicly?

Only if you give separate, written consent. It is off by default.

Can I use my own music?

Yes, if you can show a licence for commercial use. Without proof, I use my own or licensed audio. Trending sounds are not included — add them in the app when you post.

What if I send fewer than 4 hooks?

The price stays USD 299 and I deliver the number of hooks you send, each in both formats. Extra hooks beyond four cost USD 55 each.

What if you can’t deliver on time?

I work individually. If something prevents me from delivering on time, I’ll tell you before the due date, and the refund rule for late delivery applies: you may choose a refund of the undelivered part. If I ever bring in another editor, I’ll tell you first.

How many clients do you take at once?

I work individually and keep capacity limited: at most 2 active monthly plans and 1 sprint at a time, and only 1 Scale plan. If I’m full, I’ll tell you before you pay.
