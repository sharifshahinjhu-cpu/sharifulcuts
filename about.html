About

I’m Md. Shariful Islam, a short-form video editor.

I work individually from Bangladesh (UTC+6). I started as a self-taught editor, built a Top Rated Upwork profile with a 100% job success score, and in 2026 I narrowed my work to two offers: Authority Shorts and Ad Creative Sprint.

How I work

  • Each offer is a written specification: what you get, in which formats, by when, for what price.
  • Before I edit, I check your files and answers against a written input list, and tell you what I understood.
  • Before I send anything, I check every file against the specification (format, size, captions, audio, text placement).
  • I tell you if something isn’t a fit, and if I’m full I tell you before you pay.
  • If I ever bring in another editor, I will tell you first.

What I don’t do

  • Scripts, hook writing or ad copy
  • Posting to your accounts, or running and testing ads
  • Guaranteed views, sales or ad results
  • Trending sounds (you add them in the app when you post)
